## 1. Who we are and scope

1.1 This Privacy Policy explains how LevelUp Ecosystem, San Diego, California, USA (postal address available on request) ("**LevelUp**", "**we**", "**us**"), collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information.

1.2 **When we are the controller / business.** This Policy applies to personal information we collect, as controller (GDPR) or "business" (California), through:
- our website levelup-ecosystem.com and subdomains;
- LevelStudio (studio.levelup-ecosystem.com);
- the client Dashboard (dashboard.levelup-ecosystem.com) — for account and user information of our clients' staff;
- our communications, sales, support, billing and events.

1.3 **When we are a processor / service provider.** When we host and operate websites for our clients ("**Client Sites**"), the personal information collected from visitors and customers of those sites (for example, booking requests, contact messages, guest lists) is controlled by **our client**, not by LevelUp. We process it only on our client's instructions under a data processing agreement. The client's own privacy policy applies. If you are a customer of one of our clients and want to exercise privacy rights, please contact that business; if you contact us, we will forward your request to them where appropriate.

## 3. How we use personal information

We use personal information to:
1. **Provide the Services** — create and manage accounts, build and host websites, generate LevelStudio previews, operate the Dashboard, deliver support.
2. **Communicate** — respond to inquiries, send service, security, billing and legal notices, and project updates.
3. **Process payments and keep business records** — invoicing, collections, accounting, tax.
4. **Secure the Services** — authenticate users, prevent fraud, spam and abuse, rate-limit, detect and investigate security incidents, enforce our Terms and Acceptable Use Policy.
5. **Improve the Services** — understand aggregate usage, fix bugs, measure performance, develop new features (using aggregated or de-identified data where feasible).
6. **Marketing** — send newsletters or offers about our own services, with an opt-out in every message (and with consent where required by law). We do not use third-party advertising networks to target you.
7. **Comply with law** — respond to lawful requests, maintain records, establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

**AI processing.** When you use LevelStudio or AI-assisted features, your inputs (prompts, business information and any images you upload) are sent to third-party AI model providers to generate content. Where available, we select provider settings or terms under which your inputs are not used to train the provider's general models; however, provider practices vary and may change. **Do not enter personal information about other people, or confidential information, into LevelStudio prompts.** We do not use your inputs to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

6.5 **Authorized agents.** You may use an authorized agent. We will require written, signed permission from you and may ask you to verify your identity directly, unless the agent holds a valid power of attorney under the California Probate Code.

6.6 **Shine the Light (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83).** We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct-marketing purposes.

6.7 **Do Not Track.** Browsers' "Do Not Track" signals have no common industry standard; we do not respond to them as such, but we honor GPC signals as described above.

## 11. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the risk, including: HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit; encryption at rest provided by our infrastructure providers; role-based access controls and row-level security in our database so each client can access only its own data; the principle of least privilege for staff and contractors; multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts; secrets management; logging and monitoring; automated backups; dependency and security updates; and vendor due diligence. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and authorities as required by law.

## 12. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy, including:
- account information: for the life of the account, then up to 3 years for legal claims and records;
- LevelStudio previews and prompts of free users: up to 12 months after last activity, then deleted or de-identified;
- billing and tax records: 7 years;

- marketing consent records: as long as needed to demonstrate consent;
- client-site data processed on behalf of clients: per the client's instructions and our DPA (normally deleted 30 days after termination, backups rolling off thereafter).
