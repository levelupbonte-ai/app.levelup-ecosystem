# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

**LevelUp Ecosystem**
Effective date: October 11, 2026
Last updated: October 11, 2026

---

> ### The short version
>
> - For the personal data of your customers, visitors and staff that flows through your LevelUp website and Dashboard, **you are the controller and LevelUp is your processor** (in California terms: you are the "business", LevelUp is your "service provider").
> - We process that data only to provide the services and on your documented instructions — never to sell it, never to advertise to your customers, never to train AI models of our providers where we can prevent it.
> - Our staff are bound by confidentiality; we use security measures listed in Annex 2; we use sub-processors by category (Annex 3) and give you notice before adding new ones.
> - We help you answer data-subject requests, notify you of a personal-data breach without undue delay, and return or delete your data when the service ends.
> - EU/UK transfers to the U.S. are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

---

## 1. Scope and precedence

1.1 This DPA forms part of the Master Services Agreement or Terms of Service (the "**Agreement**") between LevelUp Ecosystem ("**LevelUp**", "**Processor**") and the client ("**Client**", "**Controller**").

1.2 It applies whenever LevelUp processes Client Personal Data on behalf of Client. "**Client Personal Data**" means personal data contained in Client Data. "**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws applicable to the processing, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("**GDPR**"), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss FADP, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("**CCPA**"), other U.S. state privacy laws, and PIPEDA.

1.3 If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement regarding the processing of Client Personal Data, this DPA prevails. If the Standard Contractual Clauses apply and conflict with this DPA, the Standard Contractual Clauses prevail.

## 2. Roles and instructions

2.1 Client is the controller (or business) and LevelUp is the processor (or service provider) of Client Personal Data. Where Client is itself a processor for a third party, LevelUp is a sub-processor and Client warrants that its instructions are authorized by the relevant controller.

2.2 LevelUp will process Client Personal Data only on Client's documented instructions, which are: the Agreement and Orders; Client's configuration and use of the Services and Dashboard; and other reasonable written instructions consistent with the Agreement. LevelUp will inform Client if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws, and may suspend the affected processing. LevelUp may also process data where required by law, after informing Client unless the law prohibits it.

2.3 Client is responsible for the lawfulness of the collection and of its instructions, including providing privacy notices, obtaining consents (including for cookies on Client Sites where required) and having a legal basis.

## 4. LevelUp's obligations

4.1 **Confidentiality.** LevelUp ensures that personnel and contractors authorized to process Client Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and access it only as needed for the purposes in Section 8.1 of the Terms of Service.

4.2 **Security.** LevelUp implements the technical and organizational measures in Annex 2, appropriate to the risk (GDPR Art. 32). LevelUp may update them provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

4.3 **Sub-processors.** Client gives general authorization for LevelUp to engage sub-processors in the categories in Annex 3. LevelUp will (a) impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this DPA in substance; (b) remain responsible for their performance; (c) maintain a current list available on request; and (d) notify Client by e-mail (or Dashboard notice) at least **14 days** before adding or replacing a sub-processor. Client may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period; the parties will discuss in good faith, and if no resolution is reached Client may terminate the affected Services and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

4.4 **Data-subject requests.** Taking into account the nature of processing, LevelUp will assist Client by appropriate technical and organizational measures (including Dashboard export and deletion features) to respond to requests to exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. If LevelUp receives a request directly, it will forward it to Client without undue delay and will not respond except to direct the individual to Client, unless legally required.

4.5 **Assistance.** LevelUp will provide reasonable assistance with Client's data-protection impact assessments and prior consultations, to the extent required by law and relating to LevelUp's processing. Assistance beyond the standard features of the Services may be billed at LevelUp's hourly rate.

4.6 **Personal data breach.** LevelUp will notify Client **without undue delay, and where feasible within 48 hours**, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Client Personal Data, with the information reasonably available (nature of the breach, categories and approximate numbers affected, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed), supplementing it as it becomes available. LevelUp will take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the breach. Notification is not an admission of fault. Client is responsible for notifying regulators and individuals where required, with LevelUp's reasonable assistance.

4.7 **Return and deletion.** On termination, LevelUp will make Client Personal Data available for export for 30 days, then delete it from active systems, and from backups on their normal rotation (currently within 35 days), unless retention is required by law — in which case LevelUp will protect it and process it only for that purpose. LevelUp will not condition the return of Client Personal Data on payment of outstanding fees.

4.8 **Audits.** LevelUp will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including this DPA, its security documentation and, where available, third-party certifications or audit reports of its key sub-processors. If that is insufficient, or a regulator requires it, Client may conduct an audit (by itself or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality) no more than once per year, on at least 30 days' notice, during business hours, without access to other clients' data, at Client's cost (including LevelUp's reasonable time at its hourly rate).

## 5. AI processing

Where the Services use AI model providers (for example, to generate or translate website content), LevelUp will (a) send only data reasonably necessary for the feature; (b) where available, use provider settings or terms under which inputs are not used to train the provider's general-purpose models and are retained only for a limited period; and (c) treat such providers as sub-processors under Section 4.3. Client should not include personal data in AI prompts unless necessary.

## 6. International transfers

6.1 LevelUp is located in the United States, and Client Personal Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where LevelUp or its sub-processors operate.

6.2 **EEA.** To the extent Client Personal Data subject to the GDPR is transferred to LevelUp in a country without an adequacy decision, the parties incorporate by reference the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, **Module Two (controller to processor)** (and Module Three where Client is a processor), with: Clause 7 (docking) included; Clause 9 option 2 (general authorization, 14 days' notice); Clause 11 optional language omitted; Clause 13 — the supervisory authority of Client's EU establishment or EU representative; Clauses 17 and 18 — the law and courts of Ireland. Annexes I–III of the Clauses are completed by Annexes 1–3 of this DPA. Where LevelUp or a sub-processor is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, it may rely on that certification instead.

6.3 **UK and Switzerland.** For UK transfers, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU SCCs (version B1.0) applies; for Swiss transfers, the SCCs apply with references to the FADP and the Swiss FDPIC as required.

6.4 LevelUp will ensure onward transfers to sub-processors are protected by an appropriate transfer mechanism.

## 7. CCPA service-provider terms

With respect to Client Personal Data subject to the CCPA, LevelUp: (a) will not **sell** or **share** it (as defined in the CCPA); (b) will not retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Agreement, or as otherwise permitted for service providers by the CCPA and its regulations; (c) will not retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship between LevelUp and Client; (d) will not combine it with personal information LevelUp receives from other sources, except as permitted by the CCPA regulations (for example, for security and fraud prevention); (e) will comply with the CCPA and provide the same level of privacy protection; (f) will notify Client if it can no longer meet its obligations; and (g) grants Client the right, on notice, to take reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use. LevelUp certifies that it understands and will comply with these restrictions.

## 8. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in the Agreement, except where Data Protection Laws or the Standard Contractual Clauses provide otherwise.

## 9. Term

This DPA remains in effect as long as LevelUp processes Client Personal Data.

## Annex 2 — Technical and organizational measures

1. **Encryption:** TLS (HTTPS) for all data in transit; encryption at rest provided by infrastructure providers.
2. **Access control:** role-based access; organization-scoped data isolation enforced by database row-level security; least-privilege access for personnel; multi-factor authentication on administrative and provider accounts; prompt revocation of access for departing personnel.
3. **Authentication:** managed authentication provider; hashed passwords; secure, HTTP-only session cookies where applicable.
4. **Secrets management:** server-side secrets kept out of client code; publishable keys only in browsers; key rotation after suspected exposure.
5. **Application security:** input validation, security headers, protection against common web vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10), anti-spam/bot measures on forms, rate limiting.
6. **Availability and resilience:** reputable cloud infrastructure; automated backups; documented restore process; uptime monitoring.
7. **Change management:** version control, code review where feasible, staged deployments, dependency updates and vulnerability monitoring.
8. **Logging and monitoring:** access and error logs retained for a limited period; alerting on anomalies.
9. **Vendor management:** sub-processors selected for their security posture and bound by written data-protection terms.
10. **Incident response:** documented process for identifying, containing, assessing, notifying and remediating incidents.
11. **Data minimization and deletion:** collection limited to what the features require; deletion on request and at termination.
12. **Personnel:** confidentiality agreements; security awareness.
