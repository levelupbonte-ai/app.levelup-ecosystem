# Acceptable Use Policy

**LevelUp Ecosystem**
Effective date: October 11, 2026
Last updated: October 11, 2026

---

> ### The short version
>
> - Use LevelUp websites, LevelStudio, the Dashboard and our platform only for lawful business purposes.
> - No illegal goods or services, fraud, phishing, malware, spam, hate or harassment, infringement, unlawful adult content, or attempts to hack, scrape or copy our code and infrastructure.

## 1. Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") applies to everyone who uses the Services of LevelUp Ecosystem ("**LevelUp**"), including client websites hosted on the LevelUp platform ("**Client Sites**"), LevelStudio, the Dashboard, APIs and the LevelUp tag. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). Clients are responsible for ensuring that their staff, contractors and the users of their Client Sites comply with this AUP to the extent they control them.

## 2. Prohibited content

You may not use the Services to create, host, publish, transmit, promote or sell content that:

2.1 **Is illegal** under the laws applicable to you, your users or LevelUp, including U.S. federal and California law.

2.2 **Offers illegal or highly regulated goods or services without required licenses and controls**, including: illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia; controlled substances or prescription drugs without valid prescription processes; firearms, ammunition, firearm parts or explosives where sale is unlawful or without required age and license verification; counterfeit goods; stolen goods; human trafficking or sexual services; unlicensed gambling; unlicensed financial, lending, investment, money-transmission or cryptocurrency services; and goods or services subject to sanctions.

2.3 **Is age-restricted without appropriate controls** — for example, alcohol, tobacco, vaping, cannabis (where legal) or adult content — unless the Client implements age-gating and complies with all applicable laws. Sexually explicit content is not permitted on Client Sites unless agreed in writing with LevelUp in advance; child sexual abuse material is strictly prohibited and will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and law enforcement.

2.4 **Is fraudulent or deceptive**, including scams, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, fake reviews or testimonials, impersonation of another person or business, misleading health or financial claims, fake "official" government services, and undisclosed paid endorsements.

2.5 **Infringes intellectual property or privacy rights**, including copyrighted text, images, video, music or software used without permission; trademark or trade-dress infringement; and publication of private information about others without a lawful basis (doxxing).

2.6 **Is hateful, harassing or violent** — promotes violence, terrorism or violent extremism, threatens or harasses individuals, or attacks people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, veteran status or other protected characteristics.

2.7 **Is defamatory** or knowingly false and damaging to another person or business.

2.8 **Exploits minors** in any way.

## 3. Prohibited activities

You may not:

3.1 **Phishing and credential theft** — create pages or forms that imitate another organization, or collect passwords, payment data or personal information under false pretenses.

3.2 **Malware** — distribute viruses, ransomware, spyware, cryptominers, malicious scripts, or links to them.

3.3 **Spam and unlawful messaging** — send unsolicited bulk e-mail or SMS, or use the Services (including booking confirmations or contact forms) to send messages that violate the CAN-SPAM Act, the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, the GDPR/ePrivacy rules, or similar laws; buy or use purchased e-mail lists; or misrepresent sender identity.

3.4 **Attack or probe LevelUp or others** — attempt to gain unauthorized access to any account, system, database or network; scan, probe or test vulnerabilities without our prior written authorization (see Section 6 for responsible disclosure); bypass authentication, row-level security, rate limits, quotas or other controls; launch denial-of-service attacks; or intercept traffic.

3.5 **Access or copy our code and infrastructure** — scrape, crawl, download, mirror, extract, decompile or reverse-engineer the source code, templates, designs, database schemas, APIs, prompts or AI pipelines of LevelUp, LevelStudio, the Dashboard or Client Sites; or use automated means to access the Services except through documented APIs within published limits.

3.6 **Abuse the Services** — create accounts using false identities or automated means; create multiple accounts to evade quotas, free-tier limits or suspensions; place excessive load on the Platform; use storage or bandwidth for file distribution, backups or hosting unrelated to your Client Site; or use AI features to generate content prohibited by this AUP.

3.7 **Resell without permission** — resell, sublicense, white-label, or offer the Services, LevelStudio output or Client Sites to third parties as your own product, unless you have a written reseller or agency agreement with LevelUp.

3.8 **Build competing products** — use the Services, LevelStudio output or LevelUp Materials to develop, train or benchmark a competing website builder, template library or AI model.

3.9 **Violate privacy laws** — collect, store or process personal data without the notices, consents and legal bases required by law; process sensitive data (health, biometric, children's data, payment card numbers, government IDs) unless expressly agreed with LevelUp in writing.

3.10 **Interfere with others** — disrupt other clients' sites or users, or use the Services in a way that could expose LevelUp or other clients to blocklisting, legal liability or reputational harm.

## 4. AI-specific rules (LevelStudio and AI features)

You may not use AI features to: generate content that violates this AUP; create deepfakes or impersonations of real people; generate content intended to mislead consumers (for example fake reviews); attempt to extract system prompts, model configurations or other users' data ("prompt injection"); or input personal data of others or confidential information you are not authorized to share.

## 5. Enforcement

5.1 **Investigation.** We may investigate suspected violations, including by reviewing content and logs as permitted by our Terms (Section 8) and Privacy Policy. We are not obligated to monitor content.

5.2 **Actions.** Depending on severity, we may: warn you; require removal of content; remove or disable content ourselves; restrict features; suspend a Client Site or account; terminate the Services; and report activity to law enforcement or affected parties.

5.3 **Notice.** Where reasonable, we will notify you and give you an opportunity (normally 7 days) to cure. We may act **immediately and without prior notice** where we reasonably believe there is illegal activity, a security threat, risk of harm, or a legal obligation to act, and will notify you afterwards when lawful.

5.4 **No refunds for violations.** Fees are not refunded for suspension or termination caused by a material AUP violation, except where required by law.

## 6. Reporting abuse and security issues

- **Abuse reports:** contact@levelup-ecosystem.com, subject "Abuse Report", with the URL, a description, and any evidence.
- **Copyright complaints:** send a notice including the elements required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) (identification of the work, the infringing material and its location, your contact information, a good-faith statement, a statement of accuracy under penalty of perjury, and your signature) to our copyright contact: LevelUp Ecosystem, Attn: Copyright Agent, contact@levelup-ecosystem.com (subject "Copyright Notice"; postal address available on request). We may remove material and notify the affected client, who may submit a counter-notice. We terminate repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** report privately to contact@levelup-ecosystem.com, subject "Security Disclosure". Do not access data that is not yours, degrade service, or publicly disclose before we have had a reasonable time to fix the issue. We will not pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who follow these rules.

## 7. Changes

We may update this AUP to address new risks or legal requirements. Material changes will be notified as described in the Terms.
