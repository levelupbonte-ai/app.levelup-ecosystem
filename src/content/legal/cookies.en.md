## 1. Scope

This Cookie Policy explains how LevelUp Ecosystem ("**LevelUp**") uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage, session storage, pixels, scripts) on levelup-ecosystem.com, studio.levelup-ecosystem.com and dashboard.levelup-ecosystem.com. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

**Client Sites.** Websites we build and host for clients may use their own cookies (for example booking, embedded maps, or analytics chosen by the client). The client is responsible for its own cookie notice and consent; LevelUp can provide a consent banner as part of the platform. Contact the business operating that site for details.

## 2. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. "Local storage" and "session storage" are similar browser features that store data on your device. Some are "first-party" (set by the site you visit) and some "third-party" (set by another domain). Some last only for your session; others persist until they expire or you delete them.

## 3. Categories we use

**3.1 Strictly necessary (always on).** Required for security, sign-in, load balancing, remembering your consent choice and basic preferences. They cannot be switched off in our tools, but you can block them in your browser — some features will then stop working. Legal basis in the EEA/UK: they are exempt from consent because they are strictly necessary for the service you request.

**3.2 Analytics and performance (optional).** Help us understand aggregate usage and performance. We prefer cookieless, privacy-respecting analytics that do not identify you or track you across sites. On levelup-ecosystem.com we use a cookieless web-analytics and performance-measurement service provided by our hosting provider. It sets no cookie and stores nothing on your device; it records page views, the referring site, your approximate country, browser and device type, and page-speed measurements, without creating an identifier that follows you across sites, and the query string of the page address is removed before recording. Any analytics tool that would use cookies or device identifiers would be loaded **only after you consent**, with IP truncation and without advertising features.

**3.3 Functional / personalization (optional).** Remember optional choices, such as interactive demo states or form drafts.

**3.4 Advertising and cross-site tracking.** **We do not use them.**

## 5. Your choices

- **Cookie settings:** use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer to accept, reject or customize optional categories at any time. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
- **Global Privacy Control (GPC):** if your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a refusal of optional analytics and as an opt-out of any sale or sharing.
- **Browser controls:** all major browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data.
- **Do Not Track:** see the Privacy Policy.
